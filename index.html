FYI: I am employing a software development practice I developed called Search Coding.

This practice employs 100% human written code, 100% human-led optimization, and a documentation-first , error message analysis approach. LLMs are only employed as a "Glorified Search Engine of Last Resort", hence the name "Search". It is only used for explaining and/or determining the causes of errors with/without possible solutions when no such easily searchable solution exists.

This is different from...

Vibe Coding which use agents for the full development of a program. Traditional/Purist approach where LLMs are not used at all, relying on pure documentation/textual resources. Hybrid approach where repititive tasks are delegated to LLMs, as well as (possibly) making LLMs the primary and first option for references.

Search Coding then leverages the benefits of traditional/purist approach for knowledge retention and mastery of the "Art of Programming", and the capacities of LLMs to scour the internet for information.
